## Context

Software AG is an enterprise software vendor. This engagement is mine directly rather than delivered
through Jaden Data, and it covers two things at once: moving the company off SaaS into its own
environment, and building AI capability that stays with the teams afterwards.

## Business problem

SaaS is a recurring bill, and the systems in question did not need to run there. The second half of
the problem was not cost at all. AI practice that lives in a deck leaves when the consultant does;
what the teams needed was capability they hold themselves, in the systems they already work in.

## My mandate

Move the company from SaaS to on-premises, and build AI skills across the teams together with best
practices in their internal systems.

## Decisions

- **Move the systems into the company's own environment.** The migration off SaaS is the cost
  decision, and the result is stated in subscription cost alone — more than €180,000 — with nothing
  else folded into the figure.
- **Build the capability inside the teams, not beside them.** The AI skills went to the people doing
  the work, across more than one team, rather than to a central group the others would queue for.
- **Put the practices where the work happens.** The best practices went into the company's own
  internal systems, so they sit in the place the teams already open every day.

## Delivery

Two tracks ran side by side: the migration off SaaS, and the enablement work inside the teams. No
dates, duration or team size are published for this engagement.

## Outcome

The migration saved more than €180,000 in SaaS costs alone. The teams came out of it with AI skills
of their own, and with the practices written into the internal systems they work in every day.

## Evidence

The €180,000 is a saving in the client's own subscription spend, reported by them, so it is labelled
a customer estimate rather than measured. The capability outcome carries no number and is stated as
qualitative. No dates, duration, team size or technology list is published for this engagement.
