## Context

Software AG is an enterprise software vendor. This engagement was mine directly — not delivered
through Jaden Data — and it covered two things: a migration from SaaS to on-premises, and the AI
skills the teams needed to hold themselves.

## Business problem

Running on SaaS is a recurring bill, and the systems in question did not have to run there. The
second half of the problem was capability rather than cost: AI practices that belong to the teams
and sit in the systems they already work in.

## My mandate

Move the company from SaaS to on-premises, and build AI skills across the teams together with best
practices in their internal systems.

## Decisions

- **Migrate, and count the saving narrowly.** The systems moved from SaaS to on-premises, and the
  result is stated in SaaS subscription cost alone — more than €180,000 — with nothing else added to
  the figure.
- **Build the skills across the teams.** The AI capability went to the people doing the work, in
  more than one team.
- **Put the practices where the work happens.** The best practices went into the company's own
  internal systems, so they sit in the place the teams already open every day.

## Delivery

Nothing beyond this is published about the engagement: no dates, no duration, no team size and no
technology list. What is stated here is the scope I was given and what it produced.

## Outcome

The migration from SaaS to on-premises saved the company more than €180,000 in SaaS costs alone. The
teams came out of it with AI skills of their own, and with the AI best practices written into their
internal systems.

## Evidence

The €180,000 is the client's own cost. It is labelled a customer estimate rather than measured
because it is an effect on the client's spending, reported by them, not a figure counted in my own
project or company records. The capability outcome carries no number and is stated as qualitative.
No scope, date, team size or technology beyond the two lines above is published, so none is claimed
here.
