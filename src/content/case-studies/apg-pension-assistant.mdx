## Context

APG administers pensions for a large share of the Netherlands. Written pension communication is hard
to follow for members who are not native Dutch speakers.

## Business problem

Members needed to understand what a letter says, what the difficult terms mean and what they have to
do, in a language they read comfortably, without a call-centre interaction for every letter.

## My mandate

I designed the solution architecture and the AI processes, wrote the prompts, and was accountable
for design quality and for delivering to the customer's expectations.

## Stakeholders

Member-communication owners, internal user groups and a UX validation group providing continuous
feedback, IT, security and compliance, and the executive sponsors.

## Constraints

Deployment in the client's on-premises cloud under Dutch financial regulation; members with varied
language and digital skills; accuracy requirements on financial content.

## Decisions

- Assemble the first prototype in one day from standard platform components, then dedicate the
  project to system integration and user experience.
- A conversational interface backed by a six-step process (audio transcription, text generation,
  translation, text-to-speech) and a retrieval pipeline that parses incoming letters and pulls
  supporting context from a vector store.
- Twelve two-week sprints with tight coordination with the validation groups, so adoption problems
  surfaced before go-live.

## Delivery

Twelve iterations from kick-off to the MVP running in the on-premises environment.

## Outcome

Measurable improvements in accessibility and user experience for a diverse member base, and
recognition by a Dutch ministry as one of the best examples of impactful AI integration.

## Reuse

The assistant process pattern, the ingestion and retrieval pipeline and the multilingual output
components became reusable platform capabilities.

## Evidence

Prototype and sprint timelines are project records; the accessibility improvement is the client's
qualitative validation; the ministry recognition is public.
